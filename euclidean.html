THE EUCLIDEAN ALGORITHM

Let m and n be positive integers with n < m. Let 
m = nq₁ + r₁
n = r₁q₂ + r₂
r₁ = r₂q₃ + r₃
  … 
    rₙ₋₁ = rₙqₙ
be the result of iterating the Division Algorithm, where rN is the last non-zero remainder. Then:
gcd(m, n) = rₙ
The greatest common divisor and the least common multiple are related by:
lcm(m, n) = (m × n) / gcd(m, n)
The Euclidean Algorithm repeats division: m = nq + r. Replace (m, n) with (n, r) until r becomes 0. The last non-zero remainder is the GCD. Then LCM = (a x b) / GCD.
